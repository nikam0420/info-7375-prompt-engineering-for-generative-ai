About the team

The Talent Mobility team powers Stripe’s global agility by making talent movement effortless, compliant, and business-aligned. We partner with individual Stripes and stakeholders to handle travel, temporary assignments, permanent relocations, immigration, and internal career moves. By designing and managing global mobility, immigration, and internal mobility programs, we help Stripe move talent smoothly across borders, roles, and teams, unlocking opportunities for growth, enabling the business to deploy talent globally, and unleashing Stripe’s global potential.

What you’ll do

The Immigration Specialist supports the administration and coordination of Stripe’s U.S. immigration programs, with a primary focus on employment-based green card cases and additional support for nonimmigrant visa matters. This role manages day-to-day program activities, helps ensure cases are handled accurately and in line with established policies and legal guidance, and supports a smooth and transparent experience. The specialist works closely with Stripes, managers, internal partners, immigration counsel, and external vendors to deliver timely, high-quality immigration support.

Responsibilities

Coordinate U.S. employment-based permanent residence cases from initiation through key milestones, in partnership with immigration counsel, and review case information and documentation for completeness, accuracy, and consistency before submission to counsel

Track case progress, deadlines, dependencies, and required actions, and follow up with Stripes and partners to keep cases moving and on track

Oversee regular case audits to confirm records are accurate, complete, and aligned with established immigration processes and documentation requirements, and prepare regular reports on case volume, status, aging, deadlines, and program trends

Support manager enablement by providing clear guidance, resources, and timely updates that create a smooth and transparent immigration process for managers

Support nonimmigrant visa matters, including H-1B, L-1, O-1, TN, and E-3 cases, based on program needs

Use technology and artificial intelligence (AI) tools to identify recurring issues and improve processes, communications, and case workflows

Contribute to immigration projects, audits, vendor coordination, and program documentation

Demonstrated curiosity and initiative, including a willingness to ask thoughtful questions, seek context, and learn unfamiliar processes or subject areas, and the ability to work effectively through ambiguity, balance competing deadlines and priorities, and adapt quickly as business needs or circumstances change
